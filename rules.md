# Server Rules

Play fair and keep it fun for everyone. Breaking these may result in a warning, kick, or ban.

## General

- Be respectful — no harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.
- No cheating, hacked clients, exploits or duping.
- No griefing, stealing, or destroying others' builds.
- Keep chat friendly — no spam, excessive caps, or advertising.

## Gameplay

- Don't claim land you won't use; leave room for others.
- PvP only where it's allowed.
- Report bugs and exploits instead of abusing them.
