# Documentation

Helpful links, guides and mod wikis for the modpack. *(Work in progress — more coming soon.)*


## Getting started

- Controls & keybinds:
  - Select your Starter Pokémon: C
  - Open Pokémon Party: C
  - Change selected Pokémon: ↑ ↓
  - Open Pokémon PC: P
  - Zoom: X
  - Map: M
  - Jukebox: J
  - Accessories: H
  - Voice chat settings: V
  - Claim protection settings: '
  - Ping: Middle mouse button
  - Waypoints: Ux
    
- How to evolve a Pokémon: meet requirements → "C" to access your Pokémon summary → "Evolve"
- How to create a party: /openpac-parties create *your_party_name*
- How to join a party: /openpac-parties join *party_name*
- How to claim a land *(must be on Towns/Resources map)*: "M" to open map → select area holding right-click → claim

## Featured mods

- [Cobblemon Wiki](https://wiki.cobblemon.com/) — catch, train and battle Pokémon in Minecraft.
- [Cobbleverse Wiki](https://www.lumyverse.com/cobbleverse/why-should-i-play-cobbleverse/) — defeat gym, league and unlock new regions in Minecraft.
- [Legendary Monuments Wiki](https://legendary-monuments.gitbook.io/legendary-monuments-wiki/) — explore, summon and catch legendary Pokémon in Minecraft.
- [Open Parties and Claims Wiki](https://modrinth.com/mod/open-parties-and-claims) — create a clan, claim lands, protect your builds *(only available on Towns & Resources map)*.
- [Sophisticated Backpacks Wiki](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/sophisticated-backpacks) — carry more, upgrade, add widgets.
- [Plasmo Voice Wiki](https://plasmovoice.com/) — proximity voice chat, meet new people.
- [Xaeros Minimap Wiki](https://modrinth.com/mod/xaeros-minimap) — view map, minimap, add waypoints and plan your adventures.
